About us

DietSurvival is a Minecraft survival server focused on providing a fun, friendly and long-term experience. Whether you enjoy building, trading, grinding, PvP or playing with friends, the server gives you plenty to do without losing the classic survival feeling.

Enhanced Survival SMP - Build, trade and explore.

  • 💰 Balanced Economy: Buy, sell and build your wealth.
  • 📜 Quests: Complete objectives and earn rewards.
  • 🎁 Ranks & Crates: Unlock useful rewards and perks.
  • ⛏️ Silk Touch Spawners: Collect spawners for your farms.
  • ⚡ Quality of Life: /tpa, /back, shops and more.

Community Gameplay - More ways to have fun.

  • ⚔️ Custom PvP Arena: Fight without losing your valuable items.
  • 🏟️ Mob Arena: Battle mobs and earn rewards.
  • 🎯 Events: Take part in new server activities.
  • 🛒 Player Economy: Trade and progress with other players.

Server FAQs.

Quick answers to common questions about DietSurvival.

How do I join the server?

Java: add play.dietsurvival.fun. Bedrock: use play.dietsurvival.fun with port 27779.

What gameplay features are available?

Survival, economy, quests, crates, shops, Silk Touch spawners, Mob Arena, custom PvP, teleportation, /back and events.

Can Bedrock players join?

Yes. Use play.dietsurvival.fun and port 27779.

How do I support the server?

You can vote for the server, invite friends, participate in the community and use the store when available.

I have another question. Where can I ask?

Join the DietSurvival Discord and contact the community or staff team.
