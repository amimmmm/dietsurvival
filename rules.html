SERVER RULES

Please read the rules before playing. They are here to keep DietSurvival fair, friendly and enjoyable for everyone.

DietSurvival Rules

Breaking the rules may result in warnings, mutes, temporary bans or permanent bans depending on the situation and severity.

01

Respect

Respect all players and staff. Harassment, bullying and insults are not allowed.

02

Impersonation

Impersonating another player or a member of the staff team is prohibited.

03

Scamming

Scamming or intentionally deceiving other players is strictly prohibited.

04

Discrimination

Racism, discrimination, hate speech and offensive content are not allowed.

05

Chat

Do not spam or flood chat. Keep conversations respectful and appropriate for the community.

06

Inappropriate Content

NSFW content, nudity, sexual content, self-harm content and inappropriate usernames or skins are prohibited.

07

Politics

Political and highly controversial discussions are not allowed on the server.

08

Suspicious Links

Phishing links, IP grabbers, malicious files and other suspicious links are prohibited and may result in a permanent ban.

09

Pinging

Do not excessively ping players or staff. Repeated abuse may result in a mute or timeout.

10

Advertising

Do not advertise other Minecraft servers, Discord servers, YouTube channels, server IPs or referral links.

11

Stealing & Griefing

Do not steal from or grief player builds, even when they are unclaimed. Naturally generated structures may be modified.

12

Gameplay Structures

End Portals and similar important structures may be claimed, but they must remain accessible to other players.

13

Claims

Keep your claims at least 150 blocks away from other players' claims unless you have their permission.

14

Hacking & Exploits

Hacks, exploits, autoclickers, macros and bug abuse are prohibited. Report bugs to staff instead of abusing them.

15

Real-Money Trading

Trading in-game items or currency for real money is prohibited. Both the buyer and seller may receive a permanent ban.

Important

Staff may take action against behavior that harms the community even if that exact behavior is not specifically listed above. If you find a bug or exploit, report it to staff instead of using it.

RESPECT • PLAY FAIR • HAVE FUN
