Getting Started

New to DietSurvival? Follow these quick guides to join the server, create your account and learn the most useful survival features.

Portal to Overworld

When you join DietSurvival, head to the portal area at spawn to begin your survival adventure.

  • Find the glowing portal at spawn.
  • Walk into the portal to enter the Overworld.
  • You can also speak to the Tutorial NPC nearby if you need help.
  • Once teleported, explore the wilderness and start building your base.
TUTORIAL

Shop

Use the DietSurvival shop to buy useful blocks, items and resources for your survival journey.

Type /shop to open the shop GUI and browse available items.

Type /sell to open the sell menu and sell your unwanted items for money.

What you can do:

  • Buy blocks, tools, food and resources
  • Sell unwanted items using /sell
  • Use an easy-to-navigate shop GUI
  • Earn money and progress through survival
i
Tip!

You can earn money by selling items you don't need. Use /sell to turn resources into cash.

TUTORIAL

Quests & Crate Keys

Complete quests on DietSurvival to earn rewards and crate keys.

  • Use /quest to view your available quests.
  • Complete the required objectives to earn rewards.
  • Use earned Quest Crate Keys at the crate area in spawn.
  • You can also get Voter Crate Keys by voting for the server.

🎮 How to Join

Java Edition

Server IP: play.dietsurvival.fun

Bedrock Edition

Address: play.dietsurvival.fun
Port: 27779

🔐 Register & Login

First time joining:

/register <password> <password>

Returning players:

/login <password>

🌀 Teleport to Friends

Send a teleport request:

/tpa <player>

Accept a request with the server's teleport accept command.

💀 Return After Death

Return to your previous location with:

/back

⚔️ Custom PvP Arena

Enter the custom PvP arena with:

/warp pvp

Practice combat against other players without risking your valuable survival items.

💰 Shop, Quests & Crates

Use the server's economy, complete quests and collect crate rewards to progress through DietSurvival.
